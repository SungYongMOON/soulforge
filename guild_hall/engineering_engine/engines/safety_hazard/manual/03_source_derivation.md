# 03. Source derivation

The package's source packet records the official DLA locator, revision, access class, and
SHA-256 for the direct-read MIL-STD-882E Change 1 source image. It retains only locators and
short paraphrases in Git; raw source bytes remain outside Git.

The direct derivation maps §§4.3.2-4.3.8 and the hazard-tracking context to candidate evidence
checks. It does not claim that the standard applies to an unbound project. The historical NASA
NPR source is explicitly obsolete and cannot execute a rule. RAG is retrieval support only and
cannot establish source status, applicability, acceptance, or a verdict.
