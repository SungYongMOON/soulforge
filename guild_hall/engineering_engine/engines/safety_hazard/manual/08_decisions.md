# 08. Decision boundary

Human Owner authority is the only route for accepting residual risk. An engine, AI agent,
Profile, source packet, RAG result, exact reference, fixture, or passing test has no authority to
accept risk, approve a mitigation, validate a delegation, or close a hazard.

E07's positive state means only that the configured evidence fields are present under its
candidate deterministic contract. It is not an acceptance, closure, compliance, release, or
production decision.
