# Owner decisions and holds

The following cannot be inferred by this package and require an exact Project Binding plus the
appropriate Human Owner/project authority:

1. whether either NASA/GSFC source applies and any tailoring/precedence;
2. reliability, maintainability, availability, supportability, and closure targets;
3. selected models, FMECA method/criticality definitions, units, time window, data cutoff, and
   source revisions;
4. authority to approve a plan, decide risk/closure, authorize a repair/release, or procure a
   spare; and
5. use of a paid, controlled, private, customer, or project source.

When an input or source does not close one of these, the engine must retain `UNKNOWN/HOLD`.
Evidence present without authority does not become satisfaction, and public source access does
not become project applicability.
