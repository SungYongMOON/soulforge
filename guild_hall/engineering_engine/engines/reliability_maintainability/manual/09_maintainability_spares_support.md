# Maintainability, spares, and support

The package separates three questions that often get collapsed:

- a maintainability demonstration is formal repair-simulation evidence compared with a bound
  requirement;
- a maintenance concept says what/where/how maintenance is to be carried out; and
- logistics support analysis links support resources, spares, support equipment, and the
  operational concept to readiness.

Neither a support-analysis reference nor a spare-analysis reference authorizes a part selection,
purchase, inventory action, support contract, repair action, or fielding decision. If a project
needs procurement or supply-chain analysis, that is a separate domain/authority boundary.
