# RAG and source retrieval

RAG may locate an official source or a candidate evidence reference. It is not
source authority and cannot fill a missing drawing, BOM, instruction, training
record, inspection plan, material status, or calibrated-equipment fact.

If the direct source, revision, binding, or fact is unavailable, the engine
holds the relevant facet. The package does not cache raw source body text in
public Git or in receipts.
