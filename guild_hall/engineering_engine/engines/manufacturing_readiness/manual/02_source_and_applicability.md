# Source and applicability boundary

The source packet records three directly verified public NASA standards and one
MRL guidance locator. NASA standard applicability is conditional on the
relevant NASA or contract/agreement binding. Without that binding, the engine
must retain `UNKNOWN/HOLD` rather than apply the standard to another project.

The MRL locator is taxonomy/retrieval context only. It cannot create a rule,
source revision, or assessment result. Paid and controlled source bodies are
listed as gaps, never used as implied support.
