# Facets and vocabulary

The vocabulary is closed to drawings, BOM, processes, tooling, work
instructions, personnel and equipment prerequisites, inspections, and
materials. The BOM facet is a task-authorized projection of a declared
parts/material list. It is not a claim that an official source uses the term,
nor an inventory lookup.

Every request carries each facet exactly once. Facet labels are not aliases;
an unknown label or a duplicate is refused before evaluation.
