# 03 — Source derivation

The direct source inventory is [the source packet](../contracts/calibration_measurement_validity_source_packet_v0.md). It uses official NIST and ILAC public locators only. The implementation extracts only a narrow, public-safe semantic boundary:

- traceability is not inferred merely from an instrument being calibrated;
- no universal interval is generated by the engine;
- uncertainty and conditions remain explicit facts; and
- a recalibration interval remains an upstream program decision.

ISO/IEC 17025 is cited only as a controlled-standard boundary. Its body was not read or copied. No rule is an executable compliance clause from that standard.

Search or RAG may find sources, but cannot enter the evaluator as authority or facts.
